#sci [[The Virtual]], while digital does in fact take physical space in [[Sci The Space Station]]. This means that there are disasters and phenomena that can damage its infrastructure and very being can be damaged. 


Just like the real world, [[The Virtual]] is not immune to disaster. You can climb to high ground for a flood; escape a burning building; find heat and shelter for a blizzard. This is not the case for a virtual world. Unlike you, a virtual world cannot foresee disasters. How can one defend themself from a corruption? Let alone a family, building, block, district.
