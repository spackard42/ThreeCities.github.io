#dungeon #location #apoch 
As long as there has been [[Apoch]] there within it was *The* Dungeon. It is a route down to the very center of Apoch. Dedicated to [[Life and Death|God of Death]], this dungeon has grown with the historic layers of Apoch, ever deepening as the world grows layer by layer, adding floors each time.  Death uses it part as a remembrance of all that has passed and as a planned gift to [[Life and Death|God of Life]] as a dedication and remembrance of their past.
