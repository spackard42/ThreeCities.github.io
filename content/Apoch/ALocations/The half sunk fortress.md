Set on a colossal crab, this fortress is owned by [[The queen's pirates]] as their main headquarters. Instead of using it as an offensive power it is instead used as a elusive hideout to lay low after action. It gets it's name by the lower sections which are typically sunken at all times. Perfect for members that have developed gills. the crab has it's own ecosystem, that with the pirate's  agricultural care and worship to their God has been able to thrive.
