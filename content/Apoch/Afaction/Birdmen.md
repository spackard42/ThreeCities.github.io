Delivery men, Called for their use of pigeons and to gather mail locally.
Jobs:
* Postlord
	* Man in charge of the Birdmen guild, organizes and ensures the guild's standings and performance across the lands.
* Delivery men
	* people that carry the mail across an individual torn.
*  Fowlers
	* delivery men that travel between towns and outposts bringing mail and birds from afar.
* Pigeons
	* messengers that work for high ranking members of society.
* Postmasters
	* Locals that are in charge of their local Birdhouses (see in property)
* Post Mages
	* Magic users that specialize in magical transmission of letters and whatnot. Often serve high level members of society such as local rulers.
* Hawks
	* men trained in arms that hunt down stolen packages

# Property/land
In each town they own a "birdhouse" at their outpost, where they house pigeons for locals to take home and use to send mail to the post house, or other nearby towns.
Positive relationships with most authorities:
	High tension with [[Wroughters]] at times depending on their control: if wroughters have great control of a town it is unlikely they will much tension. But if it is a town they are actively expanding into they are likely to target the Birdmen, ensuring silence from reinforcements of local towns and protections.
