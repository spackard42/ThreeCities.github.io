#faction Those that manage dungeons near towns to ensure monsters don't overrun towns. Expressly inspired by Senshi from Dungeon Meshi.

Typical responsibilites:
* Note those that go into the dungeons
	* Bringing back their bodies
	* 
* cleaning the higher floors
	* design ecosystems that ensure its safer above and that creatures inside don't invade above
* Aid in mapping dungeons
* Dungeon licensing: interview and process people interested in going into "their" dungeons. They give out licenses for guarantees of help, not having one can mean being denied revival.
	* tagging giving makers to identify bodies: Often done with simple trinkets and the like. Typically use things like distinctive "lucky jewerly" of cheap stones and whatnot. This fact is an open secret as they also are expected to record the initial gear going with them as well.
