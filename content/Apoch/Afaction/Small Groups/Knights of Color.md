#faction 
A group of powerful magic knights that are empowered by [[Crown]]'s glass. Each Knight has a specific color. They tend to adventure. When [[Crown]] rests they have full control of their given armor and weapons, however upon awakening knights have lost their titles to others by the order of Crown, which is why there are alternate names for some knights.
