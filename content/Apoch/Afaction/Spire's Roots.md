Underling mages and wizards serving in the creation of [[The Spire]]. Most are serving for simple personal gain and the freedom that comes with their service.
> Who else but the builders and architects choose which heaven is reached.

# Ranks
Flesh
	Below the bottom and never included in the vision of the spire's creation. They are bodies that do physical work. Some wizards are capable of great feats of levitation and other works. But the material must often come from elsewhere. Often necromancers will harvest corpses for this work but it is done by living too. Sometimes a poor man's body is cheaper than the components to rise a dead form to work.
	The standard is to keep note of groups and simply number the workers within: such as first last, the 29th division, with 50 workers.
mana pools
	bottom of the pyramid. Mistreated and often worked to their death in one form or another. Their 2 forms of work is as living casks of mana or simpler spell weavers, creating material from magic. A most taxing art of magic. They are promised this is training, and that with time and mastery they shall rise in rank.
