Apoch, a fantastical world of gods, myth and magic. its deep history is recorded in the ancient structures buried beneath. Worship and cults are aplenty, though each god has their own relationship with their followrs.
# The Gods
Apoch is a deeply magical world due to its vast (in power and number) gods . Gods are leveled in their power by their generation, and their impact and "humanity" and directness of action is typically characterized by their broadness:
1st and foremost above all is Apoch themself. The tales of Apoch taking lower forms are rare but in the creation myths of the 2nd generation. The 2nd Generation, also known as [[The Old Storms]] are rare to take their "person" forms, and tend to remain in a form of stasis or rest, no longer actively crafting the world as they were want to do.

# Food 
Wheat based breads are common. Communal soups are popular for their cheap cost and adaptability to ingredients and longevity. Meat is eaten in 2 forms, cooked the day of sale/butcher, with the remaining meat salted and jerked.
