#beings Angels are ethereal and highly magical beings that tend honor, embody or worship a specific god 
Their shape is wildly variable, ranging forms of people, objects and animals. They can often be distinguished by their "holiness" including but not limited to holy powers, forms that are "more" than what they seem to be based off.
