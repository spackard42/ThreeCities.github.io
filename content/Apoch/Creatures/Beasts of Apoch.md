* Sirens
	* Terrifying tempters of the waters, they lure people into waters to be drowned and devoured. The younger of the species are "small" enough to live in waters: these young replicate the sound of young children drowning (reference to [Ahuizotl](https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahuizotl_(mythology))). There are common tales from sailors of fools that fall victim to their songs in the seas.
* Nymphs
	* often categorized by their nature, these spirits are embodiments of local environments and elements.
	* dryads, naiads, etc.
* Groundhawks
	* Birds of prey infused with stone. Primarily use ambush tactics. Known to shed and fling their feathers off in defense.
* Drowned
	* Undead that have died in the swamp, their unrest has left them hungry and violent
* Lightning Serpents
	* Gigantic electric eels. Known to eat well sized sharks and other fish
* Rosebug ![[Rosebug.jpg]]
	* predator bugs that hunt small bugs and creatures
* Swamp Kings
	* gigantic herons whose legs are the width of entire tree trunks. Known to eat men as well as other animals ranging in size from small dogs to occasionally creatures the size of cattle. Their primary diet is gargantuan frogs, alligators and large fish. Though rare to behold, they are capable of flight, their flapping can be mistaken as small storms and heavy gusts of wind.
