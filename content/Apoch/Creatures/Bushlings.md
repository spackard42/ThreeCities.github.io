Small, fast moving plant creatures. There have been instances of them being seen using tools; Leading some philosophers and wise men to argue they should be included as #beings. But the argument is still ongoing and their inclusion breaks multiple long standing rule s of required sentient species to be animals in nature, or otherwise categorized as magical beings: That being an animal first and foremost, as they are plants. And that tool use is not a determinant to true intelligence, such as ground-hawks using stones to hunt.
