#peoples originating from the [[Isles of Beasts]]. Despite the drastic differences in size of their supposed ancestors all Animal Folk share a fairly similar size; between 3-4 feet on the average.
# Variations
* Mammals
	* cats, dogs and foxes
	* rodents
* Birds
	* corvid
	* birds of prey
	* small birds
	* waterfowl
* Lizards
	* tortoise
	* alligator/crocodile(?)
- (eventually) Bugs
	- Mantids
	- Spiders
	- grasshoppers
	- ladybugs
	- etc.
NOT INCLUDED
- snakes
- microscopic organisms
- fish
- mollusks
