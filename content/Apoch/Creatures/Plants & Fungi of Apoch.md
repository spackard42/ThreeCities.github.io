## Trees
* Grove/swamp trees
* The Watching Trees (Birch)
	* Birch Trees with eyes that follow movement. Possibly a superorganism
	* https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%ADr_na_n%C3%93g 
* Grasping sprigs
	* a bush sized carnivorous plant that eats small to medium sized animals such as rabbits and fawn.
* Bloodwood
	* rare tree that supposedly grows atop the local of a murder in the woods. Its sap is bloodlike in look and viscosity. Predators are known to scratch and eat from its sap. Some use it to grow leeches.
## Fungi
* Witches Cauldron 
	* A rare mountain delicacy, based on the real thing. Their interior liquid is a nutritional blessing beloved by locals as a [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarcosoma_globosum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarcosoma_globosum)
* The choral haunt
	* a heavily gilled mushroom that naturally creates sound when wind passes through said gills. With a large enough cluster they can naturally create chords.
* Cursed Remnant
	* A magical mushroom that grows from cursed composting material. Its caps resemble faces frowning in agony. Often feared for the fact it is a bioindicator of cursed locations.
* Wizards beard
	* lichen that glows a gentle blue. Often collected and broken down for its glowing for practical uses.
* Bakers Bark
	* iceland lichen https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cetraria_islandica. This leaf-like lichen is often used in small towns for breads, porridges, soups, etc.
* Trees' treat
	* a lichen that develops dews of sugary sap used to entrap bugs. Enjoyed, especially in Teas.
* dryads bed
	* a soft ground lichen, occasionally used as clothing liner for lumberjacks and dryads.
* coughing shrooms
	* mushrooms with a poisonous spore that they release in clouds.
