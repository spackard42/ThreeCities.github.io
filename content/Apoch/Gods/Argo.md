#gods God of Fire and Industry
* Related to the dragon god [[Sagyr]]
Since the first civilization, Argo has been there cultivating his people. Argo is not just a force of power but a teacher; fire heats, but burns too. A technology can crush as many lives as it can save.
# Followers
His cult, [[Embers of Argo]] is obsessed with fire and industry and progress. Known to offer their service to the highest bid.
