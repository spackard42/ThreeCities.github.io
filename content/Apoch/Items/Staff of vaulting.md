#item #magic staffs capable of stretching their length. With greater precision on their magic they are capable of lengthening farther. Some downsides can occur in mishaps of the magic: such as gaining weight with its growth (sometimes twisted into a benefit as they can be shrunken to be far more hideable at a shrunken state) or worst of all, weakening of the staff, wherein vaulting heights becomes far more dangerous if the staff is overwhelmed by weight or active cut from an adversary.

# Qualities
Minor - capable of expanding up to a foot in length.
great - capable of stretching up to 15 feet
mythic - capable of stretching 30 ft
