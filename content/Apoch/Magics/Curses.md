# Cocooning:
in which one us enclosed in an object, commonly some living material such as plant, carapace, shell and bone or an inanimate material such as crystal, rock,  metal and ice. While one might find it initially helpful from the brunt of attacks it will slowly limit the mobility until the cursed is doomed to being stuck in place.
# Thorns 
a painful curse in which one becomes covered in thorny vines, often growing out of them. Sometimes self inflicted as a guarantee of retribution when hurt.
# Symbiosis 
A strange curse in which some tool, item, clothing magically latches onto someone afflicted. Typically the curse begins by wielding/wearing the item.

# Curse of Vision/Prophecy
those capable of prophesizing. This curse is not limited to sight alone despite the naming. More severe cases are often unable to partake in daily life, and as such limit their interactions either by isolating or limiting the senses they prophesize: often clothing that signifies the risk, such as a mask of clay hands that cover the eyes.
