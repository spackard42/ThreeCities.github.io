# Forms
All magic has a basis in which form it requires to manifest, some often even require multiple:
- wards - written, carved or shaped magics
- Music of the spheres - musical magic and that of the word
-  contortion(?)
- Trade & Sacrifice: the destruction or events of one thing to cause the spell, such as a crystal or blood being destroyed to create the spell.
- movement: similar to music in nature. Precise movements are required like the movement of a hand
- alchemical: 

Apotropaic magic - wards of defense, often based off prayers to the gods for protection. Generic magics do exist but specified ones become far stronger with the obvious downside of a required specificity to their effects.
