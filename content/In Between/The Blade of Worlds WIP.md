#Anomaly A blade capable of slicing across reality, creating small (and importantly temporary) portals across The Cities

possible names
* Reality Cutter
* Distance Blade
* Sandalphon (angel reference)
Either currently in the hands of [[Perennial]] or [[Diana]], Perennial has the goal of studying it to possibly fragment it so they can easily move across realities.
