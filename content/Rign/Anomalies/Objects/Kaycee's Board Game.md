#Anomaly A cursed version of Kaycee's Board Game[^1].

The game is a deck based game, each card has an expected token cost between three resources: either in blood, bones, or special-artifacts/holy.
Farm animals are free to play
* Chicken 1 blood, 1 bone, flip a coin and return card to hand at next round if heads
* Goat: 3 blood 1 bone, cost 1 blood
* Cow: 2 blood 2 bone, cost 1 blood
* Villager: 3 blood 2 bone 1 holy, cost 1 blood
