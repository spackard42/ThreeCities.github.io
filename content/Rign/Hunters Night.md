# The first Hunters Night
Before the first night, in a court of law; One of the most popular men in a small town was charged with the murder of a vampire and the breaking of curfew. He was previously set to become knighted for his popularity, but the murder was to be met with execution. His first claim of defense was that he had been invited to the public night, and such had in no way broken the curfew, and that the victim had attacked him first.

He argued that he had just as much right to be out and hunt at night as vampires did. Taking a devious joy from the argument, [[The Baron]] responded that he would have a proving night, curfew would be extended and that the town would be encouraged to watch the great game.

The man died, hunted by a family member of the vampire he killed. He died along with one vampire in the incident. But outside of the event 17 vampires had disappeared and 7 villagers[^1] were later found dead with signs of struggle that pointed to fights with vampires.
