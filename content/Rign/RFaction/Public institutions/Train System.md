# stallion station 
This station was named after a folktale of a clever horse that bucked off and killed a greedy and cruel knight after he killed the horse's stable keeper. On top of the Station is a marble statue of the horse. Once a year an effigy of the knight is put atop the horse to be destroyed by the public.
