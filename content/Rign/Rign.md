# Peoples
While the people of Rign are not as unique as The Virtual's and technically singular unlike Apoch's many folk and kinds, the people (comparable to the human in apoch) can become monstrous: [[Vampires]], [[Werewolves]] and more exist. However there seems to be some supernatural veil that tends to hide these changes, and the [[Triangle Agency]] and the [[Bureau of Anomaly Containment]] spend a great amount of resources on hiding the many [[Minor Anomalies]] that take place

# Food & Drink
Grain breads are common and considered in all meals.
meat from pigs, cows and chickens are common enough.

Classic breakfasts include grain based cereals, oatmeals or toast with eggs or another source of protein.


## Drinks
With advancements in plumbing and sanitation water is safe to drink. 
fruit drinks are available but typically the fruit is imported from different countries.
### Alcohol
The countries relative agricultural focus on grains means that the country is renowned for its alcoholic drinks of beers and lager.

Lager was originally developed here by vampires in caves as a drink. Such as the renowned [[Lager Caves]].
