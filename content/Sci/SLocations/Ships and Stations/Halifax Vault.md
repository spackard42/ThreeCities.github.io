#sci #ship A seed and life vault station hidden within [[The Belt]]. It had become secluded and became a lost secret during [[The Collapse]] as it's then 17 scientists vowed to hide away to protect the life it was preserving from fear that the station may be destroyed or robbed during the ensuing violence that occurred.

# Sections
## The Vaults
the primary purpose of the station. A vast maze of  hallways filled with cryo-shelves storing seeds, DNA, and spores in a unique and complicated organizing system. 
## The Greenrooms
 A dozen small greenhouse rooms capable of simulating the vast variety of environments the seeds in the station kept to ensure none would decay over time and allow testing on them against diseases and other research. Each is about the size of a walk in closet. Four are designed specifically to produce water submerged environments. The other eight provided more solid soil environments.
## Housing
### "The Tombs"
15 Bedrooms nicknamed for their provided slide out beds capable of cryogenic storage. These small rooms roomed 2 personnel each with a separate bed, desk and a shared large closet.
## the easery
A dining room that also provided more casual recreational activities such as a variety of holographic board games and two TVs screens built into the walls. Also features a small glass room that was capable of playing zero-g based games.
