A medium sized personal travel and cargo ship. Abandoned during [[The Collapse]]. These ships have now become luxury vehicles for [[Canners]]. As they feature built in housing capabilities for travel across [[Home's Cradle|Portal Gates]]. 
By default their living quarters include: 
- a sleeping bunk 
- bathroom with working shower
- a small table room for meals and socializing
- a small kitchen
