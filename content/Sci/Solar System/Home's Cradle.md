#tech #location A portal gate to the rest of the galaxy, destroyed during [[The Collapse]]. It now is haunted by automate who use the remaining infrastructure of the gate to rebuild and create more automata. No one knows their exact directives, but some fear they will bring about the final destruction of [[Sci The Space Station]].

"Cradles" are the name given to the teleportation gates that allow interstellar travel. Typically they arrive to human landmarks in two ways: 
- They are sent over space, taking hundreds or thousands of years and partially manned by crews and androids
- Locations with resources are sent blueprints and instruction to build the gate and given breaks in their returns for the investment of instant transmission. If done properly the shipments of raw material is never noticed, as the cradle is finished before the final long-distance delivery.
