#beings
With the cruelty of space mankind has bioengineered macro sized tardigrades approximately the size of small livestock such as goats or pigs and are used for meat. Their creation was to have livestock that  would be guaranteed survive in travel and in breaches in space.

Their taste is typically unpleasant on it's own, with some theorizing that this taste partially comes from their diet of algae.

There are stories of nightmare incidents of giant tardigrades escaping containment and causing havoc and death in other stations but none are confirmed to be true.
